## Q1. Can you detail your experience with backend technologies like Nest.js, Node.js, and database management?

### Answer

In my role at Cybage, I utilized Nest.js, a Node.js framework, for building scalable backend services. Nest.js’s modular architecture and use of TypeScript help enforce strong typing and maintainable codebases, which enhances long-term project stability.
I have also worked extensively with Node.js for developing RESTful and GraphQL APIs that support high-concurrency environments typical in fintech domains.
Regarding database management, I have hands-on experience with both relational databases like MySQL, PostgreSQL, and NoSQL databases like MongoDB and Redis. I design schemas optimized for query performance and transactional integrity, particularly important for financial data.
My approach includes leveraging caching strategies with Redis to reduce database load and improve response times. I consider database scalability and replication to ensure fault tolerance and responsiveness globally.
Integration of backend with cloud storage and serverless components further enhances scalability, fault tolerance, and cost optimization, aligning well with Mastercard’s technology stack and high-availability demands.
