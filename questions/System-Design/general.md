Q1. Describe your experience working in Agile teams and cross-functional collaboration?

Participation in daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and retrospectives helped keep the team synchronized and improved productivity.
My understanding of Agile principles allowed me to prioritize tasks effectively, break down complex features into manageable user stories, and deliver high-quality increments on time.
I actively contributed to knowledge sharing and mentoring junior developers, fostering a collaborative and growth-oriented environment.
